About
Eitan “Eddie” D’vir is the Editor-at-Large of JBN and the host of the JBN Unfiltered podcast. He began his career as a photojournalist, later expanding into full investigative and political reporting. His work covering Jewish and Israel-related affairs began in college, when he conducted an early interview with Deputy Prime Minister Natan Sharansky, an experience that helped shape the direction of his journalistic career.

D’vir has reported extensively on Israeli politics, security, and Jewish communal life, interviewing prime ministers, Knesset members, senior defense officials, scholars, and community leaders. During the Second Intifada, he was the first journalist to arrive at the Sbarro pizza bombing in Jerusalem. His photographs from the scene were published widely in national Israeli outlets, including Yediot Aharonot and Maariv.

His work also includes documenting the voices of Holocaust survivors and, in more recent years, survivors and families affected by the October 7th attacks, ensuring their testimonies, experiences, and resilience are recorded with accuracy and respect.

D’vir has reported from the White House press room and has been invited to and reported from the White House Chanukah Party, covering matters relating to U.S.–Israel relations and the rise of antisemitism in the public sphere and on university campuses.

As host of JBN Unfiltered, D’vir conducts in-depth, on-record conversations with policymakers, frontline defenders, survivors, security and intelligence professionals, cultural figures, and voices shaping the global Jewish conversation today.

His journalism is rooted in the belief that the Jewish people’s story, both past and present, must be documented with truth, dignity, and historical clarity.
